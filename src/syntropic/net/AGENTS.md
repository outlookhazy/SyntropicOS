# Networking & Secure Transport Invariants (`src/syntropic/net/`)

Applies to Ethernet/IP (`syn_eth`, `syn_icmp`, `syn_igmp`, `syn_autoip`, `syn_dhcp`, `syn_udp`, `syn_tcp`, `syn_netbuf`, `syn_netcfg`, `syn_router`), secure transports (`syn_tls`, `syn_dtls`, `syn_wg`), and application protocols (`syn_dns`, `syn_dnssd`, `syn_sntp`, `syn_ntp_server`, `syn_http`, `syn_httpd`, `syn_websocket`, `syn_mqtt`, `syn_coap`, `syn_heartbeat`).

## Cross-Directory Touchpoints

- **Crypto, Sched & Proto**: `src/syntropic/crypto/*` (TLS 1.3, DTLS 1.3, WireGuard), `src/syntropic/pt/syn_pt.h`, `src/syntropic/sched/syn_sched.h` (`PT_CONTINUE_IF_NEEDED`, `PT_SPAWN_IF_NEEDED`), and `src/syntropic/proto/syn_lwm2m.h` (`syn_lwm2m_task_pt`).
- **Transports & Ports**: `syn_transport.h` (`syn_transport_tcp`, `syn_transport_udp`), `src/syntropic/port/syn_port_socket.h`, `src/port/syn_port_eth.h`, and `src/port/posix/port_posix_socket.c`.

## Protocol & Security Invariants

- **Untrusted Wire Bounds Before Pointer Arithmetic**: Validate minimum frame sizes and variable header offsets against actual received length *before* indexing payloads — specifically IPv4 `IHL * 4` options offsets, TCP data offset, UDP length vs. packet length, and HTTP/WebSocket header/body/chunk limits.
- **Bounded Pointer/Label Decompression (`syn_dns`, `syn_dnssd`)**: Enforce strict hop/offset bounds and backward-only/loop guards on RFC 1035 compression pointers so crafted cyclic packets cannot loop infinitely or read out of bounds.
- **Fail-Closed Handshake & Record Guards (`syn_tls`, `syn_dtls`, `syn_wg`)**: Reject `NULL` or zero-length PSK/key material; bind peer authentication to verified handshake state (`hs_valid`) and transcript hashes (rejecting reflection); enforce 64-bit AEAD sequence-number exhaustion limits and anti-replay windows; scrub ephemeral keys and active cooperative crypto contexts (`*_wipe`) on timeout, alert, or disconnect.
- **Native `SYN_PT` Handshake Protothreads (`syn_wg`, `syn_tls`, `syn_dtls`)**:
  - **`syn_wg`**: `syn_wg_task` stages Noise_IKpsk2 X25519 steps in caller-owned `SYN_WgHsWork` (`wg->hs_work` driving `syn_x25519_pt`) and alternates `wg->rx_turn` while `wg->rekey_in_progress` is active so established ChaCha20-Poly1305 tunnel RX datagrams interleave hitlessly with background rekey slices.
  - **`syn_tls`**: `syn_tls_handshake_pt` drives TLS 1.3 client/server flights and child asymmetric protothreads (`syn_x25519_pt`, `syn_p256_*_pt`, `syn_ed25519_*_pt`, `syn_x509_verify_pt`) using `ctx->hs_work` and `ctx->crypto_scratch`; `syn_tls_task` composes `syn_tls_handshake_pt` via `PT_SPAWN_IF_NEEDED`.
  - **`syn_dtls`**: `syn_dtls_handshake_pt` drives DTLS 1.3 client/server flights and child asymmetric protothreads while discarding/rolling back forged UDP datagrams without tearing down handshake state; `syn_dtls_task` and higher-layer protocol tasks (`syn_lwm2m_task_pt` over DTLS-bound `SYN_Transport`) compose `syn_dtls_handshake_pt` via `PT_SPAWN_IF_NEEDED`.
- **Per-Packet / Per-Chunk `PT_CONTINUE_IF_NEEDED` Discipline (`syn_http`, `syn_mqtt`, `syn_httpd`)**:
  - **`syn_http`**: `syn_http_client_task` executes `PT_CONTINUE_IF_NEEDED(pt, task)` after every header read slice and every body/chunk progress step (`brc >= 2` from `http_step_body`, covering both socket-readable and buffer-drain steps).
  - **`syn_mqtt`**: `poll_rx` processes at most 1 complete payload or oversized-discard packet per slice (up to 32 header/varint/discard steps) and returns `true`; `syn_mqtt_task` yields `PT_CONTINUE_IF_NEEDED(pt, task)` whenever `syn_port_sock_readable` remains true so packet bursts drain 1 packet per slice under contention and full bursts in 1 turn when uncontended.
  - **`syn_httpd`**: Task runner yields via `PT_CONTINUE_IF_NEEDED(pt, task)` between `SYN_HTTPD_READING` -> `SYN_HTTPD_DISPATCHING` -> `SYN_HTTPD_SENDING`.
- **Inner-Packet Length vs. Transport Padding (`syn_wg`, `syn_eth`, `syn_icmp`, `syn_udp`, `syn_tcp`)**: Trim outer transport/link padding using validated inner IPv4/IPv6 `total_length` headers while rejecting inner lengths that exceed received frame length or fall below header minimums. Callers supplying `tx_buf`/`frame_tx` to `syn_eth_process_frame` or `syn_icmp_process_packet` must provide `>= SYN_ETH_MAX_FRAME_LEN` (`1514B`).
- **TCP/Socket Lifecycle, Checksums & Pool ISR Safety (`syn_tcp`, `syn_udp`, `syn_netbuf`)**: Validate mandatory TCP RX checksums and sequence/ACK/RST/FIN numbers against active receive windows; emit RFC 793 `RST` on unmatched unicast non-`RST` segments; reclaim timed-out `SYN_RCVD` slots (`SYN_TCP_SYN_TIMEOUT_MS`) while preserving unread passive-`FIN` `rx_buf` bytes until drained; never spuriously wake tasks on dropped UDP datagrams; protect `syn_netbuf` freelist and `ref_count` RMWs with critical sections; verify client vs. server message roles (e.g., DHCP client rejecting stray `BOOTREQUEST` frames).
